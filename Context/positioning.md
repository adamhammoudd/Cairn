# Cairn Positioning

## What it is
A dark-themed financial dashboard combining the data depth of Yahoo Finance with the
interactive charting of TradingView, centered on an AI analysis engine that reads market
news, cross-references historical trends, and surfaces probability-weighted context on
markets, sectors, and tickers. No brokerage, no trade execution - informational only.

## Core differentiator
The AI assistant already has context on the user's actual holdings and watchlist, so it
answers questions like "how is my portfolio doing today?" without the user re-explaining
their situation each time - something a generic chatbot in a separate tab can't do.
Sourcing and methodology transparency (every output shows its inputs and reasoning) is a
structural differentiator against competitors that output bare scores/signals.

## Non-negotiable framing
Every piece of AI output - chat responses, daily briefings, probability analyses - is
informational/sourced market-level context. It is never a personalized recommendation to
buy, hold, or sell. This holds true regardless of subscription tier; Free vs. Premium differ
in depth of detail, never in honesty of caveats.

## Business model
Freemium subscription. Free: full dashboard + capped daily AI chat messages + basic-depth
analysis. Premium: unlimited chat + full methodology depth (extended historical analogs,
granular confidence data), billed via Stripe.

## Ideal user
Someone who wants portfolio-aware market context and pattern analysis without a brokerage
relationship, and who specifically values transparency (sources, reasoning shown) over
being handed a bare buy/sell signal.
