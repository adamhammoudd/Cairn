---
name: disclaimer-compliance-check
description: Use before shipping any AI-generated content, probability/analysis output, marketing copy, or UI surface that discusses stocks, portfolios, or the AI assistant's output.
---

1. Confirm the content is framed as market/sector/ticker-level analysis or informational
   context - never as a personalized recommendation ("you should buy/sell/hold").
2. If the content includes a probability or confidence output, confirm it shows: the source
   news/data it drew from, the historical analog(s) it's pattern-matching against, and an
   honest confidence/uncertainty statement - never a bare number.
3. Confirm a visible disclaimer, or a clear link to one, appears near the content.
4. Confirm any cited news/data includes source attribution and a timestamp.
5. Confirm the language holds regardless of subscription tier - Free vs. Premium may differ
   in depth of detail shown, never in how honestly the output is caveated.
6. If any check fails, or you're unsure, escalate to cfo-legal-advisor rather than shipping.
